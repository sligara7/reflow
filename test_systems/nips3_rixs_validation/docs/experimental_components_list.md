# Experimental Components List

**Beamline**: NSLS2 SIX (2-ID)
**Experiment**: NiPS3 RIXS at Ni L-edge

---

## All Physical Components in the Experiment

### Source Components
1. **EPU49 Undulator** (Elliptically Polarizing Undulator)
   - 2.0 m length, 49 mm period
   - Energy range: 400-1600 eV
   - Produces soft X-rays for RIXS

### Optical/Mechanical Components (Beamline Optics)
2. **Monochromator**
   - High-resolution energy selection
   - Resolving power: R = 17,000 - 35,000
   - Selects 853.4 eV (Ni L3 edge)

3. **Focusing Optics**
   - Kirkpatrick-Baez mirrors or similar
   - Focus X-rays onto sample

### Environmental Control
4. **Cryostat** (Closed-cycle cryogenic system)
   - Temperature: 40 K (measurement temperature)
   - Range: 10-300 K
   - Maintains sample below TN = 155 K

### Sample
5. **NiPS3 Single Crystal**
   - Van der Waals antiferromagnetic material
   - **Electronic excitation properties UNKNOWN** ← This is our knowledge gap!

### Detection System
6. **Centurion RIXS Spectrometer**
   - Ultra-high-resolution soft X-ray spectrometer
   - 50-foot-long spectrometer arm
   - Energy resolution: ~17 meV at 853 eV
   - Electron-multiplying CCD (EMCCD) detector
